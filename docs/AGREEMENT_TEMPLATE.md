for the purpose of the LLP named `[Project Name] LLP`, LLPIN `[…]` (the "**LLP**").

## 1. Purpose
1.1 The LLP is formed solely to acquire the land described in **Schedule B** (the "**Property**"), construct a building on it, lease it, and distribute net rental income to Partners.
1.2 The LLP shall not carry on any other business without a Special Resolution (Clause 9.3).

## 2. Capital contribution
2.1 Each Partner shall contribute the amount set against their name in Schedule A by `[date]` only by bank transfer to the LLP's current account `[bank, last 4 digits]`. Cash is not accepted.
2.2 A Partner's **Ownership Percentage** = their paid contribution ÷ total paid contributions, as recorded on the Platform.
2.3 If a Partner fails to pay by the due date plus 15 days, other Partners may take up the unpaid portion pro-rata; the defaulting Partner's commitment is reduced accordingly.
2.4 Additional capital calls (e.g. cost overrun beyond contingency) require a Special Resolution. A Partner who does not contribute is diluted, not penalised further.

## 3. No assured return
3.1 Partners acknowledge that **no fixed or guaranteed return is promised**. Income depends on occupancy, rent and expenses. Each Partner shares profits and losses in proportion to Ownership Percentage.

## 4. Role of the Operator
4.1 The Operator manages land purchase, approvals, construction, leasing and day-to-day operations.
4.2 Fees (all recorded on the Platform ledger):
   - Setup fee: `[1.5]%` of total project cost;
   - Construction management fee: `[6]%` of construction cost, paid per verified milestone;
   - Property management fee: `[8]%` of rent actually collected.
4.3 The Operator shall not receive any commission, discount or benefit from contractors, vendors or tenants. Any such benefit belongs to the LLP.
4.4 The Operator may be removed by Special Resolution for cause or on 90 days' notice.

## 5. Bank account & payments
5.1 All money flows through the LLP current account only.
5.2 Payments above Rs `[1,00,000]` require approval of two signatories: the Operator and a Partner elected by Ordinary Resolution.
5.3 Construction payments are released only after milestone evidence (site photos, engineer's certificate, contractor bill) is uploaded to the Platform.

## 6. Transparency Platform
6.1 The Partners agree to use the NextGen DigInvest platform (the "**Platform**") as the official record of contributions, expenses, rent, votes, maintenance and documents.
6.2 Every Partner has continuous read access. The LLP's appointed CA has auditor access.
6.3 The ledger is hash-chained; any alteration of a past entry is a material breach.
6.4 Monthly statements and quarterly distribution statements shall be published on the Platform within `[10]` days of period end.

## 7. Income distribution
7.1 Net distributable income each quarter = Rent received − operating expenses − maintenance reserve (`[10]`% of rent) − Operator fees − taxes.
7.2 Net distributable income is paid to Partners by Ownership Percentage within `[15]` days of quarter end.
7.3 The maintenance reserve is capped at `[6]` months of gross rent; excess is distributed.

## 8. Maintenance
8.1 The Operator may approve repairs up to Rs `[25,000]` per item.
8.2 Anything above that limit, or not in the annual budget, needs an Ordinary Resolution through the Platform.
8.3 Each maintenance item shall carry before/after photographs and an invoice on the Platform.

## 9. Decisions & voting
9.1 Votes are weighted by Ownership Percentage and cast on the Platform (or in writing).
9.2 **Ordinary Resolution:** more than 50% — budgets, contractor selection, rent policy, signatory election.
9.3 **Special Resolution:** at least 75% — sale of Property, borrowing, admitting a new Partner, changing the Operator, changing this Agreement, extra capital calls.
9.4 A vote stays open for `[7]` days; Partners who do not vote are counted as abstaining.

## 10. Lock-in, transfer & exit
10.1 Lock-in of `[3]` years from completion certificate.
10.2 After lock-in, a Partner wishing to exit must first offer their share to existing Partners (**Right of First Refusal**) at fair value determined by an independent registered valuer.
10.3 If no Partner buys within `[60]` days, the share may be sold to an outsider approved by Special Resolution.
10.4 On death, the share passes to legal heirs/nominee, who step into the Partner's rights.
10.5 Sale of the Property needs a Special Resolution; proceeds are distributed after repaying liabilities, by Ownership Percentage.

## 11. Information & audit
11.1 Accounts audited annually by `[CA firm]`. Partners may inspect books on 7 days' notice.

## 12. Confidentiality, KYC & compliance
12.1 Each Partner provides PAN, identity and bank proof. NRI Partners confirm compliance with FEMA.
12.2 The LLP will not solicit investment from the public.

## 13. Disputes
13.1 Disputes are resolved by arbitration under the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996, sole arbitrator, seat `[Bengaluru]`, language English.

## 14. Governing law
14.1 Laws of India; courts at `[Bengaluru]` have jurisdiction subject to Clause 13.
