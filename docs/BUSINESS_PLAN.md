# NextGen DigInvest — Business Plan

**Idea in one line:** A small group of known partners pool money into one project company, buy approved land, construct a rental building, and share the rent — with every rupee, bill, photo, vote and document recorded on a tamper-evident app that all partners can see.

> This plan is a working draft, not legal or tax advice. Have a CA and a property lawyer review the structure and the agreement before taking any money.

---

## 1. The problem we solve

| Who | Pain today |
| --- | --- |
| Salaried professionals / NRIs | Rs 25–50 L is not enough to buy land + build alone; flats give 2–3% rental yield |
| Friends / family pooling money | No clear agreement, verbal promises, no visibility into spending, fights at exit |
| Landowners / small builders | Hard to raise construction money without high-interest loans |
| Tenants (shops, PG, small offices) | Want good, maintained buildings near growth corridors |

## 2. The model

```
Partners (5–20, known people)
        │  capital contribution
        ▼
Project LLP (one SPV per building)  ◄── Operator (you) = Designated Partner + project manager
        │  buys land, builds, leases
        ▼
Rental building (PG / co-living, shops, small offices, warehouse)
        │  monthly rent
        ▼
Rent account → expenses → maintenance reserve → operator fee → quarterly payout to partners
```

### Why one LLP per project (SPV)
- Each building's risk, loan and tax stay separate.
- Land is registered in the LLP's name, not in one person's name — no "trust me" ownership.
- Ownership % = capital contributed, written in the LLP agreement and shown live in the app.
- Profit share in an LLP is not taxed again in partners' hands (LLP pays tax); confirm current rates with a CA.

## 4. How the operator (you) earns

| Fee | Typical | When |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Project setup fee | 1–2% of project cost | At land registration |
| Construction management fee | 5–8% of construction cost | Paid per milestone, only after milestone is verified |
| Property management fee | 6–10% of rent collected | Monthly |
| Optional own stake | Invest like any partner | Aligns interest |
| Exit / sale fee | 1% of sale value | Only if partners vote to sell |

Fees are written into the agreement and visible in the app ledger — no hidden margins.

## 5. Trust & transparency — the core product

1. **Escrow-style project bank account** — contributions go only to the LLP's current account; payments above a limit need two signatories (operator + one elected partner).
2. **Tamper-evident ledger** — every transaction is chained with a SHA-256 hash of the previous entry. If anyone edits an old entry, the chain breaks and every partner sees "Ledger tampered at entry #N".
3. **Milestone-gated release** — construction money is released per stage (foundation, plinth, slab 1…). Each release needs site photos, engineer's certificate and the contractor's bill uploaded first.
4. **Document vault with fingerprints** — sale deed, EC, Khata, plan sanction, agreements are hashed when uploaded; any partner can re-check the file matches.
5. **Weighted voting** — big decisions are voted by ownership %: normal ≥ 51%, special (sale, new loan, change operator) ≥ 75%.
6. **Maintenance with approval limits** — repairs under Rs 25,000 the operator approves; above that, partners vote. Every ticket has before/after photos and the bill.
7. **Automatic distribution statements** — each quarter the app calculates every partner's share to the paisa and shows the working.
8. **Read-only auditor access** — a CA can log in and verify everything.

## 6. Legal guard-rails (important)

- **Stay a private co-ownership, not a public scheme.** Pooling money from many unconnected people, managed by someone else, with promised returns can be treated by SEBI as a *Collective Investment Scheme* (Sec 11AA, SEBI Act). Keep it to known partners, invite privately, never promise fixed returns, and give partners real control (voting, information rights).
- **Do not advertise publicly** for investors.
- **Real fractional ownership at scale** (≥ Rs 50 crore asset, 200+ investors, Rs 10 L minimum ticket) belongs in SEBI's SM REIT framework — a later-stage path, not where we start.
- **Land checks:** follow the Bangalore land document checklist (title chain 30 yrs, EC, DC conversion, layout approval, A-Khata/E-Khata, RERA if applicable).
- **Tax:** LLP files ITR-5; GST applies on commercial rent above the threshold; TDS on rent if tenant is liable. CA to confirm.
- **KYC:** collect PAN, Aadhaar/passport, bank proof for every partner; follow FEMA rules for NRI partners (NRIs face limits on investing in agricultural land and real-estate business — get advice).

## 8. Risks and how we handle them

| Risk | Mitigation |
| --- | --- |
| Bad land title | Lawyer title report before token; 30-year EC; never buy B-Khata / unapproved |
| Construction overrun | 8–10% contingency; fixed-price contract; milestone payments |
| Low occupancy | Pick sites near jobs; mixed shop + room income; 6-month rent reserve |
| Partner wants early exit | 3-year lock-in, then right of first refusal to other partners at app-calculated valuation |
| Operator misuse | Two-signatory bank account, hash-chained ledger, auditor access, removal by 75% vote |
| Partner disputes | Arbitration clause (Bengaluru seat) in agreement |
